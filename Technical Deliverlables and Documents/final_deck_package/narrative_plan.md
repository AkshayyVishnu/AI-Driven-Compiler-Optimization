# DeepCompile Final Presentation Narrative Plan

## Audience
- faculty reviewers
- project evaluators
- technical peers reviewing the final submission package

## Objective
Present DeepCompile as a complete source-to-source optimization submission with clear scope boundaries, deterministic evidence, and honest reporting of current strengths and limitations.

## Narrative Arc
1. Open with the revised project definition and why the final scope is source-to-source only.
2. Show the system architecture and how orchestration changed the workflow after Week 8.
3. Present deterministic Week 13 evidence and explain the measured outcomes.
4. Close with the final deliverable package, limitations, and next engineering priorities.

## Slide List
1. Title and revised scope
2. Final submission scope
3. System architecture summary
4. Orchestration and bounded refinement
5. Deterministic evaluation results
6. Performance and bottlenecks
7. Deliverable inventory
8. Conclusion and future work

## Visual System
- warm paper background
- dark graphite text
- green, gold, and coral accent blocks
- editorial title typography with quieter body copy
- cards and metric panels as the main visual structure

## Asset Plan
- no external images are required for the final deck
- use editable shapes, panels, and typography as the dominant visual language
- keep every visible word editable in the PowerPoint file

## Editability Plan
- all slide titles, subtitles, cards, metrics, captions, and notes remain native PowerPoint objects
- preview renders and inspect records are generated during export verification
- the final `.pptx` is the only user-facing deck artifact
